What the pipeline does

① Classify — each document is identified as 價單 Price List / 銷售安排 Sales Arrangement / 成交紀錄冊 Register of Transactions.
② Extract — a type-specific extractor reads unit-level records into one unified schema.
③ Reconcile — the funnel rule (sold > selling > offered) joins units across documents; duplicates are not double-counted.
④ Report — deterministic arithmetic builds the Monday-morning report. No human touched a spreadsheet.
